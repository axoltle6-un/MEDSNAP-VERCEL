Executive Summary: MedSnap is a production-ready, mobile-first Web Application & PWA designed for instant, accurate medication identification. Users upload photos of pills, capsules, liquids, or boxes, and MedSnap's multi-modal AI cross-references 10+ global and regional registries to generate clean, 100% typo-free clinical reports with 1-click printable PDF exports.

1. Technical Stack & Key Features

  • Frontend Framework: Next.js 16 (App Router, Standalone Build), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, Framer Motion.
  • Multi-Modal AI Engine: Mistral Pixtral 12B (Vision Analysis) and LLM7 Codestral (Text Summarization & Refinement).
  • 10+ Government & Medical Databases:
    • USA: openFDA Drug Labels, openFDA Enforcement & Recalls, NIH RxNorm, NIH RxImage.
    • Pakistan: DRAP (Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan) Registry & National Formulary.
    • China: NMPA (National Medical Products Administration) & Chinese Pharmacopoeia (ChP).
    • Global Scientific: NIH PubChem Compound Database & Wikipedia Medical Generator.
  • Security & Authentication: Firebase Auth (Google OAuth + Email/Password with SHA-256 OTP tokens & constant-time verification), Bearer ID token auto-injection, IP rate limiting, and zero cross-account data leakage.

2. Built-in Monetization & Payment Engine

  • Freemium Subscriptions: Free Tier (1 AI scan/day + unlimited database lookups) vs Pro Tier ($19.99/mo or $99.99/yr for 4 AI vision scans/day + clinical PDF exports).
  • Multi-Payment Integration: Real Stripe checkout routes configured for Credit Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.
  • Automatic Global Sales Tax Engine: Built-in country tax calculator with preset rates for 40+ countries.

3. Post-Acquisition Growth Playbook for Buyers

  • Consumer Marketing: Run targeted Meta/TikTok ad campaigns targeting caregivers, elderly assistance groups, and chronic medication users.
  • B2B Pharmacy & Clinic Licensing: White-label the API and report generator for independent pharmacies and telehealth platforms.
  • SEO Content Scale: Generate programmatic SEO landing pages for all 10,000+ indexed FDA/DRAP drug names.
